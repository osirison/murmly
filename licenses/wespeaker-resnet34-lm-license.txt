Training data
-------------

The model was trained on the VoxCeleb2 development set, with MUSAN
(CC BY 4.0, https://www.openslr.org/17/) and RIRS_NOISES (Apache 2.0,
https://www.openslr.org/28/) used for augmentation. The terms published for
VoxCeleb are not consistent with each other, and the licence of a model trained
on it is therefore not entirely clean:

  - KAIST (https://mm.kaist.ac.kr/datasets/voxceleb/): "The VoxCeleb dataset is
    available to download for research purposes under a Creative Commons
    Attribution 4.0 International License. The copyright remains with the
    original owners of the video."
  - The licence file of that distribution states: "The copyright of both the
    original and cropped versions of the videos remains with the original
    owners. The data is covered under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
    International license".
  - Oxford VGG (https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/voxceleb/vox1.html and
    https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/voxceleb/vox2.html): "The provided
    VoxCeleb metadata is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
    4.0 International License."

Every speaker-embedding model considered for Murmly was trained on VoxCeleb2, so
this does not distinguish between them. It was reviewed and accepted when the
model was chosen (2026-10-09). The model's own licence, CC BY 4.0, is the one
that applies to the file.
